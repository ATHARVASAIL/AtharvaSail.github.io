Scope

  
Finding what the scanner missed

  
Automated tools catch the obvious. The findings that matter — chained IDORs, broken object-level authorization, business logic a scanner has no concept of — come from reading the application like the people who built it did, then asking what they assumed.

  
I test web and mobile applications, internal and external networks, REST APIs, and increasingly LLM-backed features where prompt injection and data leakage are the new injection class. Every finding ships with CVSS scoring, a working proof of concept, and remediation the development team can act on — then I retest it.

Methodology

  
How an engagement runs

  
The order matters — each phase only makes sense on top of the one before it.

  

    
01
Recon
Scope confirmation, passive intelligence, attack surface mapping.

    
02
Enumeration
Service and endpoint discovery, role and permission matrix.

    
03
Exploitation
Manual verification, payload crafting, proof of concept capture.

    
04
Chaining
Combining low findings into a demonstrable compromise path.

    
05
Reporting
CVSS scoring, business impact, remediation steps developers can use.

    
06
Retest
Verification that fixes hold and introduce nothing new.

Work

  
Tooling I built

  
Each one started as something missing on an engagement.

  


    
      
TOOL-01Engagement console

      
greysh3ll

      
A 512-case VAPT methodology console spanning ten domains — network, web, API, LLM, cloud, mobile, thick client, WiFi, source review and social engineering. Imports Nmap, Nuclei and Burp output, links findings into attack chains, and stores evidence attachments. Fully client-side, no backend.

TOOL-02Attack-path graph

      
kompromap

      
Ingests scanner output and ranks compromise paths by attacker effort per hop rather than raw CVSS — because three chained mediums often beat one isolated high. FastAPI, PostgreSQL and NetworkX behind a React and Cytoscape graph, with multi-engagement support and report generation.

      
PythonFastAPIPostgreSQLNetworkXReact

TOOL-03Scanner

Detects SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, SSRF and directory traversal, then emits PDF and JSON reports with remediation guidance. Benchmarked against Burp Suite and OWASP ZAP for detection parity.
